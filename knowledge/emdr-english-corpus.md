### Mapping of themes covered in English

- Mechanisms / neurobiology: Kapoula 2010, Boukezzi 2017×2, Rousseau 2019×2, Rousseau 2020, Verger 2020, Armand 2026.
- Comparative efficacy vs other methods (Brainspotting, TlEM, Telehealth): Masson 2017, Flatot-Blin 2023, Tarquinio 2021.
- Clinical extensions (addiction, somatization, body syndromes): Perez-Dandieu 2014, Tapia 2019, Brennstuhl 2015.
- Adaptation to specific populations (migrants, health care workers, perinatal, adolescents): Gil-Jardiné 2018, Chauliac 2025, Vignaud 2023, Caille 2023, Rolling 2024, Merg-Essadi 2023-2025.
- Theoretical modeling of the AIP model: Rydberg 2024.
